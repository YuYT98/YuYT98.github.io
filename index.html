I am a PhD candidate in Bioinformatics at the University of British Columbia with a computational background in mathematics and statistics. I was a visiting PhD researcher at Harvard Medical School & McLean Hospital. My research lies at the intersection of AI, neuroimaging, and computational psychiatry, with expertise in machine learning and deep learning. I develop and apply AI-driven methods for individualized brain modeling, including normative modeling, brain age prediction, and brain atypicality analysis, to characterize brain heterogeneity and individual-level variation. More broadly, I am interested in AI for Science and AI for Health, particularly in using computational methods to advance individualized understanding of the human brain.

Supervisors: Prof. Sophia Frangou (Primary) · Prof. Roger Tam (Co-Supervisor)
